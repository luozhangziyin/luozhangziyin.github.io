Biography个人简介

I am an undergraduate in Mechanical Engineering at Tsinghua University, working at the point where scientific theory meets engineering practice — particularly precision instrumentation, measurement, and the control of physical systems. 我是清华大学机械工程系本科生，工作在科学理论与工程实践的交汇点——尤其是精密仪器、测量，以及物理系统的控制。

I grew up in London and studied at Harris Academy Greenwich, where I sat my A-Levels four years ahead of my cohort, at fourteen — A* in Mathematics, A* in Physics, and A in Further Mathematics. With the qualifications already complete, I skipped sixth form entirely and went straight to university. The two years I spent at school in the meantime went into the UK mathematics and physics olympiad circuit, which is the part of the subject I enjoy most: a problem that cannot be answered from memory and has to be taken apart carefully instead. 我在伦敦长大，就读于哈里斯格林威治学院（Harris Academy Greenwich），十四岁时参加 A-Level，比同届学生提前四年完成——数学 A*、物理 A*、进阶数学 A。因为资格考试已全部完成，我直接跳过了预科阶段进入大学。此间留在中学的两年，我投入到英国数学和物理奥赛之中，这也是这门学科里我最享受的部分：一个问题无法凭记忆作答，只能耐心地把它拆解开来。

Most of what I know about engineering, as opposed to physics, came from building things and watching them fail in simulation. That is the appetite I brought to Tsinghua, and it is what I want to keep following. 我对工程（而非物理）的大部分认识，来自亲手搭建东西、并在仿真里看着它们失败。这正是我带到清华来的求知欲，也是我想一直追随的方向。

Current Work当前工作

I am in the first year of my Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering, and I serve as Sports Monitor for my class. Tsinghua's sporting tradition — 无体育，不清华 — has shaped my university life as much as the academic side, and I have competed in a range of events since arriving. 我现在是机械工程专业本科一年级学生，同时担任班里的体育委员。清华的体育传统——无体育，不清华——和学业一样塑造了我的大学生活；入学以来，我参加了多项体育赛事。

Alongside my coursework I have three projects on the go. The first is a robotics project: planning and simulating the motion of a multi-axis robotic arm with MoveIt 2 and Gazebo. The second is SmartCanteen, a WeChat MiniProgram for campus dining that a team of five of us entered for the Tsinghua University Freshmen Innovation Project in October 2026. The third is NeuroSync, a home neurological-monitoring prototype that tracks typing rhythm and mouse tremors as an early warning, entered for the Tsinghua Medical-Engineering Innovation Competition. 课业之外，我手头有三个项目。第一个是机器人项目：用 MoveIt 2 和 Gazebo 对多轴机械臂进行运动规划与仿真。第二个是SmartCanteen（智慧食堂），一个校园餐饮微信小程序，由我们五人团队于 2026 年 10 月参加清华大学新生创新项目。第三个是NeuroSync，一个家庭神经监测原型，通过打字节奏和鼠标震颤进行早期预警，参加了清华大学医工结合创新大赛。

Beyond the Lab实验室之外

Sport has been a constant: distance running and calisthenics. I also play music, which is the one activity I have found that reliably stops me thinking about problem sets for an hour. 运动一直是我的习惯：长跑和自重训练。我还弹奏音乐，这是我发现唯一能让我在一小时内真正不再想习题的活动。

I am most useful to anyone who wants a problem taken apart rather than answered quickly. 对于那些想把问题拆解清楚、而不是急着得到答案的人，我最能帮上忙。
