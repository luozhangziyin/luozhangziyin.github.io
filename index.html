About

Undergraduate, Department of Mechanical Engineering, Tsinghua University

Biography

I am an undergraduate in Mechanical Engineering at Tsinghua University, working at the point where scientific theory meets engineering practice — particularly precision instrumentation, measurement, and the control of physical systems.

I grew up in London and studied at Harris Academy Greenwich, where I sat my A-Levels four years ahead of my cohort, at fourteen — A* in Mathematics, A* in Physics, and A in Further Mathematics. With the qualifications already complete, I skipped sixth form entirely and went straight to university. The two years I spent at school in the meantime went into the UK mathematics and physics olympiad circuit, which is the part of the subject I enjoy most: a problem that cannot be answered from memory and has to be taken apart carefully instead.

Most of what I know about engineering, as opposed to physics, came from building things and watching them fail in simulation. That is the appetite I brought to Tsinghua, and it is what I want to keep following.

Current Work

I am in the first year of my Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering, and I serve as Sports Monitor for my class. Tsinghua's sporting tradition — 无体育，不清华 — has shaped my university life as much as the academic side, and I have competed in a range of events since arriving.

Alongside my coursework I have two projects on the go. The first is a robotics project: planning and simulating the motion of a multi-axis robotic arm with MoveIt 2 and Gazebo. The second is SmartCanteen, a WeChat MiniProgram for campus dining that a team of five of us entered for the Tsinghua University Freshmen Innovation Project in October 2026 — bilingual menu information, dietary and allergen filtering, and live seating availability, so that a new student can work out what to eat and where to sit without walking the whole campus first.
