NeuroSync — home neurological monitoringNeuroSync —— 家庭神经监测

Tsinghua Medical-Engineering Innovation Competition · team project · 未来健康黑客松 清华大学医工结合创新大赛 · 团队项目 · 未来健康黑客松

Long before Parkinson's disease or dementia is diagnosed, motor control starts to degrade in ways too subtle to notice day to day. NeuroSync turns the computer a person already uses into a continuous monitor for that decline. 在帕金森病或痴呆症被确诊之前很久，运动控制能力就开始以日复一日难以察觉的细微方式退化。NeuroSync 把人已经在使用的电脑变成监测这种退化的持续设备。

The working prototype tracks keystroke dynamics — how long a key is held, and the time between keys — together with mouse micro-tremors, builds a personal baseline, and flags what drifts from it. A gradual slowing of typing rhythm can hint at early decline, while a sudden drop in accuracy is treated as a possible stroke indicator and triggers the emergency response: smart-home lights flash and help is called. 可运行的原型监测击键动态——按键按住多久、按键之间的间隔——以及鼠标微颤，建立个人基线，并对偏离基线的变化发出警示。打字节奏逐渐变慢可能提示早期衰退，而准确率突然下降则被视为可能的中风信号，触发应急响应：智能家居灯光闪烁并呼叫救援。

The companion piece, still on the drawing board, is a wristband — a closed-loop stabiliser for essential tremor and freezing of gait. A motion sensor catches the onset of a tremor or stress spike and responds with rhythmic haptic vibration and light cues to interrupt it in real time, logging every episode for both doctor and patient. 与之配套的腕带仍在设计阶段——它是一种针对特发性震颤和冻结步态的闭环稳定器。运动传感器捕捉到震颤或应激峰值开始时，便以有节奏的触觉振动和光提示做出反应，实时打断震颤，并为医生和患者记录每一次发作。

Everything runs locally in the browser — no server, no upload. That is also the privacy position: the data never leaves the machine. 所有功能都在浏览器本地运行——没有服务器，也没有上传。这同时也是隐私立场：数据从不离开本机。

SmartCanteen — a WeChat MiniProgram for campus diningSmartCanteen —— 校园餐饮微信小程序

Tsinghua University Freshmen Innovation Project · team of five · 智慧食堂 清华大学新生创新项目 · 五人团队 · 智慧食堂

Tsinghua has so many canteens that deciding what to eat is a genuine problem — especially for a new international student, who may be reading a menu in a second language and has no way of knowing that a dish contains peanuts until it arrives. SmartCanteen is a WeChat MiniProgram that answers three questions in one place: what can I eat, where do I find it, and where can I sit? 清华的食堂太多，决定吃什么成了一个真实的问题——尤其对新生国际学生来说，他们可能要用第二语言看菜单，而且在一道菜端上来之前根本无法知道里面是否含有花生。SmartCanteen 是一个微信小程序，在一个地方回答三个问题：我能吃什么？到哪里找？哪里有座位？

The idea I find most interesting is that this is a two-sided problem. Diners cannot see what is available; canteens cannot see what is wanted. Both are caused by the same missing link — the data exists, but is never joined up. So the prototype serves diners with bilingual dish information, ingredient and allergen filtering, and live seating availability, while the same underlying data feeds an operator dashboard identifying over-production and unmet demand. 我认为最有趣的一点是，这是一个双向的问题。就餐者看不到有什么可吃；食堂看不到大家想吃什么。两者都源于同一个缺失的环节——数据存在，却从未被打通。因此，这个原型一方面为就餐者提供双语菜品信息、配料与过敏原筛选和实时座位情况，另一方面用同样的底层数据为运营看板提供过度供应与未满足需求的分析。

One design decision I would defend hardest: the system distinguishes "no allergen information available" from "verified to be free of this allergen." For anyone with a real allergy those are completely different statements, and conflating them is the kind of shortcut that makes a product dangerous rather than merely unhelpful. 我会最坚决地捍卫一个设计决定：系统把“无过敏原信息”和“已确认不含此过敏原”区分开来。对真正过敏的人来说，这两句话含义完全不同；把它们混为一谈，正是那种让产品变得危险而不仅仅是无用的偷懒。

The privacy design follows the same instinct. Seat occupancy is inferred from an overhead camera, but the question asked of the model is only "is this seat taken?" and never "who is sitting here?" — no facial recognition, raw frames processed and discarded at the edge, and only anonymous aggregates stored. 隐私设计遵循同样的直觉。座位占用情况通过顶部摄像头推断，但向模型提出的问题只是“这个座位有人吗？”，而绝不是“谁坐在这里？”——没有人脸识别，原始帧在边缘端处理并丢弃，只存储匿名的聚合数据。

I worked on this in a team of five, building the WeChat MiniProgram in WeChat Dev Tools with JavaScript on the front end and Python for the detection and data side. The poster below shows the working prototype. 我和五人团队共同完成这个项目，在微信开发者工具中用 JavaScript 开发小程序前端，用 Python 负责检测与数据部分。下方的海报展示了可运行的原型。

Cartesian motion planning and simulation for a multi-axis robotic arm多轴机械臂的笛卡尔运动规划与仿真

学生课外项目 — student extracurricular project 学生课外项目

The aim was to let a robot arm be commanded in the coordinates a human actually cares about. The arm is a six-degree-of-freedom model — seven links and six joints, defined in URDF — simulated in Gazebo and driven through ros2_control. Using MoveIt 2 I converted Cartesian target poses into inverse-kinematics solutions for the joint chain, generated the trajectories between them, and verified the motion in simulation before it ever ran on hardware. 目标在于让机械臂能够以人类真正关心的坐标来接受指令。这个机械臂是一个六自由度模型——七个连杆、六个关节，用 URDF 定义——在 Gazebo 中仿真，并通过 ros2_control 驱动。我使用 MoveIt 2 将笛卡尔目标位姿转换为关节链的逆运动学解，生成它们之间的轨迹，并在真正上硬件之前先在仿真中验证运动。

Configuring the arm meant working through the MoveIt Setup Assistant in full: the self-collision matrix, the planning group, the KDL kinematics plugin as the IK solver, and the two controllers that actually execute motion — a Joint Trajectory Controller on the robot side and a Follow Joint Trajectory controller on the MoveIt side. 配置机械臂意味着完整地走完 MoveIt Setup Assistant：自碰撞矩阵、规划组、作为逆解求解器的 KDL 运动学插件，以及真正执行运动的两个控制器——机器人侧的 Joint Trajectory Controller 和 MoveIt 侧的 Follow Joint Trajectory 控制器。

I also benchmarked the planning pipelines rather than taking the default on trust, comparing OMPL, CHOMP, STOMP, TOMP and RRT-Connect on trajectory quality and planning time. The trade-off is real: the sampling-based planners return a path quickly but produce jerky motion that needs smoothing, while the optimisation-based ones give smooth paths at a computational cost that is hard to make real-time. 我也没有盲目信任默认的规划管线，而是对其做了基准测试，从轨迹质量和规划时间上比较 OMPL、CHOMP、STOMP、TOMP 和 RRT-Connect。这种权衡是真实存在的：基于采样的规划器能快速返回路径，但产生的运动会抖动、需要平滑；基于优化的规划器给出平滑路径，但计算开销大到难以做到实时。

The part I am most pleased with is the TF work. The arm does not natively understand a point in space, so I used the TF library to carry a target from a depth camera's coordinate frame into the robot's base frame — which is what let this project link up with a separate student project using YOLO detection and a depth camera to locate objects. That collaboration is where most of my debugging time went: frames that disagreed with each other, and timestamps that did not line up. 我最满意的是 TF（坐标变换）部分。机械臂本身并不理解空间中的一个点，所以我用 TF 库把一个目标从深度相机的坐标系变换到机器人基座坐标系——正是这一点让本项目得以与另一个使用 YOLO 检测和深度相机定位物体的学生项目衔接。那次协作花掉了我大部分调试时间：彼此不一致的坐标系，以及对不上的时间戳。
